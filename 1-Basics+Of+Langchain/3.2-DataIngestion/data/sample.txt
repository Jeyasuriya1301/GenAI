Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

RAG combines a retrieval system with a generative language model. Instead of
relying solely on the parameters learned during training, a RAG pipeline first
retrieves relevant documents from an external knowledge base and then feeds
them to the model as context. This grounds the model's answers in real source
material, reduces hallucination, and lets you update knowledge without
retraining.

A typical pipeline has four stages: load, split, embed, and retrieve. First you
load raw documents from a source. Then you split them into smaller chunks that
fit inside a context window. Each chunk is embedded into a vector, and the
vectors are stored in a vector database. At query time the question is embedded,
the nearest chunks are retrieved, and they are passed to the LLM.
